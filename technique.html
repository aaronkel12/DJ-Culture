DJ Techniques

Learning DJ techniques allows you to create smooth transitions, build energy and keep your audience engaged.

Beatmatching

Beatmatching involves matching the tempo and timing of two tracks so they play together smoothly.

Mixing

Mixing is the process of transitioning from one track to another while keeping the music flowing naturally.

EQ (Equalisation)

EQ mixing uses the bass, mid and treble controls to blend tracks without the frequencies clashing.

Using Effects

DJ effects such as echo, reverb and filters can add creativity and energy to a transition when used correctly.

Hot Cues

Hot cues allow DJs to quickly jump to important parts of a track, such as an intro, breakdown or drop.

Basic Steps for Mixing

  1. Choose two suitable tracks.
  2. Match the BPM of the tracks.
  3. Beatmatch the tracks.
  4. Adjust the EQ levels.
  5. Transition smoothly between the tracks.
