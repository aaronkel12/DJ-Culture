DJ Equipment

DJ equipment allows DJs to select, mix and manipulate music. Modern DJ setups can include controllers, decks, mixers, headphones and other audio equipment.

XDJ-RX3

All-in-one DJ System

The XDJ-RX3 is an all-in-one DJ system designed for mixing music without requiring separate decks and a mixer. It provides DJs with controls for playing, mixing and manipulating tracks.
